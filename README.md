# Unbubble — Free iPhone-Only iMessage Exporter

**Export iMessage to PDF free or save iMessage to CSV, straight from your iPhone. No Mac or PC required.** Unbubble is a free, open-source iPhone app: no subscription, no account, and no paid export unlock.

[Download Unbubble free on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/unbubble-imessage-exporter/id6829522001) · [Official Unbubble page and export guides](https://thegreymirror.com/grey-mirror/unbubble) · [iPhone app source](ios/)

## Your iPhone. Your messages. Your files.

Unbubble creates the export on your iPhone using an Apple Shortcut you approve. You do not need a computer, a desktop app, a USB cable, or a computer backup to use the iPhone app.

| Export | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| PDF | A readable conversation with dates, times, sender labels, numbered messages and pages, plus available photos. |
| CSV | One message per row, with timestamp, sender, direction, text and attachment paths, ready for a spreadsheet. |
| CSV with attachments | A ZIP containing the CSV and available conversation photos and files. |

Exporting is free. Grey Mirror analysis is optional and is not required to create or keep an export.

## How to export iPhone messages without a Mac or PC

1. [Get Unbubble for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/unbubble-imessage-exporter/id6829522001).
2. Open the app and add its Unbubble Export shortcut when prompted.
3. Choose a one-to-one conversation and approve the Messages access prompts.
4. Keep the app open and the phone unlocked and connected to power while the available history is collected.
5. Choose PDF, CSV, or CSV with attachments. Save to Files or share the export wherever you choose.

Check the first and last messages, dates, and both senders in the saved export. Only history and attachments available on the device can be exported; deleted messages cannot be recovered. See the [official page](https://thegreymirror.com/grey-mirror/unbubble#export-iphone-messages-without-mac) for current iOS compatibility and conversation support.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Unbubble really free?

Yes. The iPhone app and its PDF, CSV, and attachment exports are free. No subscription, account, or Grey Mirror purchase is required.

### Do I need a Mac or PC to export iMessages?

No. The Unbubble iPhone app collects the conversation and creates the export directly on the phone through an approved Apple Shortcut.

### How do I export iMessage to PDF for free?

Install Unbubble, add its export shortcut, choose a conversation, collect the available history, and select PDF. Save the PDF to Files or share it from your iPhone.

### How do I save iMessage to CSV?

Choose CSV after collecting the conversation. Each message has its own row with sender, timestamp, and text. Choose CSV with attachments to include available files in a ZIP.

### Where do my messages go?

The iPhone app processes the conversation locally. Your exported files stay on your phone until you choose to save or share them. Read the [iPhone privacy information](ios/PRIVACY.md).

## Export first. Analyze when you want.

Unbubble is a standalone exporter from the makers of [Grey Mirror](https://thegreymirror.com/). Grey Mirror analyzes conversation history to quantify patterns in initiation, effort, reply timing, conflict, and repair, with the messages behind the findings. Its free preview and optional paid full report are separate from Unbubble's free exports.
